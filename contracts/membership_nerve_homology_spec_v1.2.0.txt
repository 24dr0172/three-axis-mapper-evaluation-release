## 1. Scope and Objective

This specification formalizes the mathematical construction of the **Membership-Defined Simplicial Nerve 2-Skeleton** and the exact algebraic calculation of **Dual Homology** ($\beta_1^{\rm graph}$ vs. $\beta_1^{\rm nerve}$).

It establishes:
1. The sample-incidence construction of the simplicial nerve directly from genuine common observed-sample intersections.
2. The mathematical proof of sufficiency of the 2-skeleton for 0- and 1-dimensional homology ($H_0$ and $H_1$).
3. The linear-algebraic computation of Betti numbers over the finite field $\mathbb{F}_2$.
4. Mandatory algebraic chain complex invariants ($D_1 D_2 \equiv 0 \pmod 2$, $\beta_0^{\rm nerve} = C$, $\beta_1^{\rm nerve} = \beta_1^{\rm graph} - r_2$).
5. Strict integer-only external node identifier contracts and deterministic internal row indexing for Phase 0.
6. Exact GUDHI persistence cross-check configurations and backend contracts.

## 2. SOURCE-DEFINED Requirements

### 2.1 The Simplicial Nerve of a Finite Collection of Sets
Let $\mathcal{V} = \{V_v\}_{v \in V}$ be a finite collection of non-empty subsets of an observed sample set $X = \{x_0, \dots, x_{N-1}\}$, indexed by a finite node set $V$. Each node $v \in V$ has a non-empty support (observed sample index set) $S(v) \subseteq \{0, 1, \dots, N-1\}$.

The **simplicial nerve** $\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{V})$ is the abstract simplicial complex whose vertices are $V$, and where a finite non-empty subset of vertices $\sigma = \{v_0, v_1, \dots, v_k\} \subseteq V$ forms a $k$-simplex if and only if the mutual intersection of their sample supports is non-empty:
$$\sigma \in \mathcal{N}(\mathcal{V}) \iff \bigcap_{i=0}^k S(v_i) \neq \emptyset$$

### 2.2 Dual-Homology Definitions
1. **1-Skeleton Graph Cyclomatic Number ($\beta_1^{\rm graph}$)**:
   The 1-skeleton of $\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{V})$ is the undirected graph $G = (V, E)$ where $E = \{\{u, v\} \subseteq V \mid u \neq v \text{ and } S(u) \cap S(v) \neq \emptyset\}$.
   Let $n_0 = |V|$, $n_1 = |E|$, and $C$ be the number of connected components of $G$. The 1-skeleton cycle rank (cyclomatic number) is:
   $$\beta_1^{\rm graph} = n_1 - n_0 + C = E - V + C$$
   $\beta_1^{\rm graph}$ counts the number of independent cycles in the 1-dimensional graph structure.

2. **Simplicial Nerve Homology ($\beta_0^{\rm nerve}, \beta_1^{\rm nerve}$)**:
   Let $\mathbb{F}_2 = \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ denote the finite field with two elements.
   Let $C_k(\mathcal{N}; \mathbb{F}_2)$ be the $\mathbb{F}_2$-vector space freely generated by the oriented $k$-simplices of $\mathcal{N}$.
   The simplicial boundary operator $\partial_k: C_k \to C_{k-1}$ is the linear map defined on basis simplices by:
   $$\partial_k([v_0, v_1, \dots, v_k]) = \sum_{i=0}^k [v_0, \dots, \hat{v}_i, \dots, v_k] \pmod 2$$
   where $\hat{v}_i$ denotes omission of vertex $v_i$.
   The homology vector spaces over $\mathbb{F}_2$ are:
   $$H_k(\mathcal{N}; \mathbb{F}_2) = \frac{\ker(\partial_k)}{\operatorname{im}(\partial_{k+1})}$$
   The simplicial Betti numbers are defined as:
   $$\beta_0^{\rm nerve} = \dim_{\mathbb{F}_2} H_0(\mathcal{N}; \mathbb{F}_2)$$
   $$\beta_1^{\rm nerve} = \dim_{\mathbb{F}_2} H_1(\mathcal{N}; \mathbb{F}_2)$$

## 3. Mathematically Necessary Consequences

### 3.1 Sufficiency of the 2-Skeleton for $H_0$ and $H_1$
**Theorem**: Let $\mathcal{N}$ be an abstract simplicial complex and let $\mathcal{N}^{(2)}$ be its 2-skeleton (the subcomplex containing all simplices of dimension $\le 2$). Then:
$$H_0(\mathcal{N}^{(2)}; \mathbb{F}_2) \cong H_0(\mathcal{N}; \mathbb{F}_2)$$
$$H_1(\mathcal{N}^{(2)}; \mathbb{F}_2) \cong H_1(\mathcal{N}; \mathbb{F}_2)$$

**Proof**:
Consider the chain complex $(C_*(\mathcal{N}), \partial_*)$ and its 2-skeleton $(C_*(\mathcal{N}^{(2)}), \partial_*^{(2)})$:
$$\dots \xrightarrow{\partial_4} C_3(\mathcal{N}) \xrightarrow{\partial_3} C_2(\mathcal{N}) \xrightarrow{\partial_2} C_1(\mathcal{N}) \xrightarrow{\partial_1} C_0(\mathcal{N}) \xrightarrow{\partial_0} 0$$
By definition of the 2-skeleton:
1. $C_0(\mathcal{N}^{(2)}) = C_0(\mathcal{N})$, $C_1(\mathcal{N}^{(2)}) = C_1(\mathcal{N})$, and $C_2(\mathcal{N}^{(2)}) = C_2(\mathcal{N})$.
2. The boundary maps $\partial_1^{(2)} = \partial_1: C_1 \to C_0$ and $\partial_2^{(2)} = \partial_2: C_2 \to C_1$ are identical.
3. For $H_0$:
   $$H_0(\mathcal{N}) = \frac{\ker(\partial_0)}{\operatorname{im}(\partial_1)} = \frac{C_0}{\operatorname{im}(\partial_1)} = H_0(\mathcal{N}^{(2)})$$
4. For $H_1$:
   $$H_1(\mathcal{N}) = \frac{\ker(\partial_1)}{\operatorname{im}(\partial_2)}$$
   Simplices of dimension $k \ge 3$ belong to chain groups $C_k(\mathcal{N})$ whose boundary operator maps $\partial_k: C_k \to C_{k-1}$ for $k \ge 3$. They map into $C_2(\mathcal{N})$ or higher and do not alter $C_0, C_1, \partial_1$, or $\partial_2$. Thus $\ker(\partial_1)$ and $\operatorname{im}(\partial_2)$ are entirely determined by $C_1, C_2, \partial_1, \partial_2$.
5. Therefore, $H_1(\mathcal{N}^{(2)}; \mathbb{F}_2) = \ker(\partial_1)/\operatorname{im}(\partial_2) = H_1(\mathcal{N}; \mathbb{F}_2)$. $\blacksquare$

**Computational Consequence**: To compute exact $\beta_0^{\rm nerve}$ and $\beta_1^{\rm nerve}$, constructing simplices of dimension $\le 2$ is mathematically complete and sufficient. Higher simplices ($k \ge 3$) are strictly unnecessary and omitted to prevent combinatorial scaling.

## 4. FROZEN PROJECT-CONTRACT CHOICES

### 4.1 Standalone Nerve Identifier Domain Contract (Phase 0)

> **Identifier Domain Contract**:  
> For Phase 0, external node identifiers supplied to the standalone nerve module MUST be **integers only**.  
> - Arbitrary strings, tuples, or heterogeneous identifier types are strictly prohibited in Phase 0.
> - Any non-integer external node identifier must be rejected with `ValueError("External node identifier must be an integer in Phase 0.")`.
> - If any node has an empty support ($S(v) = \emptyset$), the input must be rejected with `ValueError("Node with empty support provided to nerve construction.")`.

**Deterministic Internal Row Mapping**:
Let the input node identifiers be distinct integers $\{v_1, v_2, \dots, v_{n_0}\}$.
The internal zero-based row mapping is defined strictly by sorting external integer IDs in ascending order:
$$\text{sorted\_nodes} = \text{sorted}(V)$$
$$\text{vertex\_to\_row} = \{\text{node\_id}: i \mid i, \text{node\_id} \in \text{enumerate}(\text{sorted\_nodes})\}$$

*Example*: If input nodes are $\{10, 30, 55\}$, the deterministic mapping is:
- $10 \to 0$
- $30 \to 1$
- $55 \to 2$

### 4.2 Sample-Incidence Simplex Construction Contract

To eliminate naive $\mathcal{O}(|V|^3)$ triple searches, simplices are constructed via sample-incidence inversion:

1. **Sample-to-Node Inverted Index**:
   For each observed sample index $i \in \bigcup_{v \in V} S(v)$, define its internal node incidence set:
   $$M_i = \{\text{vertex\_to\_row}[v] \mid v \in V \text{ such that } i \in S(v)\} \subseteq \{0, \dots, n_0-1\}$$

2. **0-simplices ($\Sigma_0$)**:
   $$\Sigma_0 = \{ (u,) \mid u \in \{0, \dots, n_0-1\} \}$$
   Sorted in ascending order. Total count: $n_0 = |\Sigma_0|$.

3. **1-simplices ($\Sigma_1$)**:
   Each sample $i$ witnesses an edge for every pair $\{u, v\} \subseteq M_i$:
   $$\Sigma_1 = \bigcup_{i} \{ (u, v) \mid u, v \in M_i, u < v \}$$
   Sorted in lexicographical ascending order. Total count: $n_1 = |\Sigma_1|$.

4. **2-simplices ($\Sigma_2$)**:
   Each sample $i$ witnesses a 2-simplex for every triple $\{u, v, w\} \subseteq M_i$:
   $$\Sigma_2 = \bigcup_{i} \{ (u, v, w) \mid u, v, w \in M_i, u < v < w \}$$
   Sorted in lexicographical ascending order and deduplicated. Total count: $n_2 = |\Sigma_2|$.  
   *(If multiple samples $i, j$ witness the same triple $\{u, v, w\}$, the 2-simplex appears exactly once in $\Sigma_2$).*

5. **Strict Prohibition of Clique Completion**:
   $\Sigma_2$ is constructed **only** from observed sample intersections via $\bigcup_i \binom{M_i}{3}$. A triangle is **never** added via clique completion of the 1-skeleton graph.

### 4.3 Matrix Representation and Exact $\mathbb{F}_2$ Linear Algebra

1. **Boundary Matrix $D_1 \in \mathbb{F}_2^{n_0 \times n_1}$**:
   For edge $e_j = (u, v) \in \Sigma_1$ ($u < v$) for $j \in \{0, \dots, n_1-1\}$:
   $$D_{1}[i, j] = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } i \in \{u, v\} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

2. **Boundary Matrix $D_2 \in \mathbb{F}_2^{n_1 \times n_2}$**:
   For triangle $t_k = (u, v, w) \in \Sigma_2$ ($u < v < w$) for $k \in \{0, \dots, n_2-1\}$:
   Its boundary edges are $e_{uv} = (u, v)$, $e_{uw} = (u, w)$, and $e_{vw} = (v, w)$. Let $\text{edge\_to\_idx}$ be the lookup map for $\Sigma_1$:
   $$D_{2}[j, k] = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } j \in \{\text{edge\_to\_idx}[(u, v)], \text{edge\_to\_idx}[(u, w)], \text{edge\_to\_idx}[(v, w)]\} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

3. **Mandatory Chain Condition Check**:
   Before performing rank reduction, the implementation must assert:
   $$D_1 D_2 \equiv 0 \pmod 2$$
   If this assertion fails, execution must immediately raise `HomologyConsistencyError("Fundamental chain condition D1 * D2 != 0 (mod 2) violated.")`.

4. **Exact $\mathbb{F}_2$ Rank Reduction**:
   - Matrix ranks $r_1 = \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(D_1)$ and $r_2 = \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(D_2)$ must be computed via exact Gaussian elimination over Galois field $\mathbb{F}_2$ (bitwise XOR row/column reduction).
   - **Strictly Prohibited**: `numpy.linalg.matrix_rank` is **forbidden** because it computes SVD-based rank over $\mathbb{R}$, yielding incorrect topological ranks over $\mathbb{F}_2$.

5. **Exact Invariant Formulas & Consistency Invariants**:
   - Simplicial Betti-0: $\beta_0^{\rm nerve} = n_0 - r_1$.
   - Component Consistency: Let $C$ be the number of connected components of the 1-skeleton graph. The implementation must verify:
     $$r_1 = n_0 - C \implies \beta_0^{\rm nerve} = C$$
   - Graph Betti-1: $\beta_1^{\rm graph} = n_1 - r_1 = E - V + C$.
   - Simplicial Betti-1:
     $$\beta_1^{\rm nerve} = n_1 - r_1 - r_2 = \beta_1^{\rm graph} - r_2$$
   - **Mandatory Invariant Bound**:
     $$0 \le \beta_1^{\rm nerve} \le \beta_1^{\rm graph}$$
     *(Any violation triggers `HomologyConsistencyError`).*

### 4.4 Homology Backend Contracts

1. **Independent Mathematical Oracle (Unit Tests & Toys)**:
   - Direct NumPy/bitwise $\mathbb{F}_2$ Gaussian elimination operating on explicit boundary matrices $D_1$ and $D_2$.
   - Serves as the primary reference oracle for small and medium complexes.

2. **GUDHI Cross-Check Protocol**:
   When cross-checking with GUDHI `SimplexTree`:
   ```python
   st = gudhi.SimplexTree()
   for u in range(n_0):
       st.insert([u], filtration=0.0)
   for (u, v) in Sigma_1:
       st.insert([u, v], filtration=0.0)
   for (u, v, w) in Sigma_2:
       st.insert([u, v, w], filtration=0.0)

   st.compute_persistence(
       homology_coeff_field=2,
       min_persistence=0,
       persistence_dim_max=True
   )
   betti_numbers = st.betti_numbers()
   # beta_0 = betti_numbers[0] if len(betti_numbers) > 0 else 0
   # beta_1 = betti_numbers[1] if len(betti_numbers) > 1 else 0
   ```
   - **Required Setting**: `persistence_dim_max = True` is mandatory so that maximal-dimension homology (such as $H_1$ of a 1D hollow triangle where no 2-simplices exist) is computed.
   - **Prohibited Setting**: Never call `st.set_dimension(2)` artificially when $\Sigma_2 = \emptyset$. The complex dimension must reflect actual inserted simplices.

3. **Production Backend Status**:
   - `OPEN IMPLEMENTATION DECISION — MUST BE CLOSED BEFORE FREEZE`
   - Candidates: Pinned GUDHI `SimplexTree` vs. custom bit-packed sparse $\mathbb{F}_2$ solver.
   - Both backends must agree identically across all unit and validation tests.

### 4.5 Separation of Mathematical Contract and Provisional Runtime Guardrails

1. **Milestone-Locked Mathematical Contract**:
   - Sample-incidence nerve construction.
   - Exact $\Sigma_0, \Sigma_1, \Sigma_2$ definitions.
   - Field $\mathbb{F}_2$, matrices $D_1, D_2$, exact rank formulas, and consistency identities.
   - Independent $\mathbb{F}_2$ Gaussian elimination oracle and GUDHI cross-check.

2. **Provisional Runtime Guardrails**:
   - The following resource ceilings are **provisional runtime guardrails** for early validation, subject to calibration during Phase 2 benchmarking:
     - Provisional Max 0-simplices ($n_0$): $10,000$
     - Provisional Max 1-simplices ($n_1$): $100,000$
     - Provisional Max 2-simplices ($n_2$): $200,000$
   - If a ceiling is exceeded during construction or matrix allocation:
     - Return `status = "resource_failure"`, log warning `RESOURCE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.
     - Invariants: $\beta_1^{\rm graph}$ remains valid; set $\beta_0^{\rm nerve} = \text{NA}$, $\beta_1^{\rm nerve} = \text{NA}$.

## 5. Non-Negotiable Reporting Contracts

1. **Dual Homology Contract**:
   Every table, log, and manuscript statement reporting homology must report **both** $\beta_1^{\rm graph}$ and $\beta_1^{\rm nerve}$, or clearly specify which invariant is reported.
2. **Never Report Bare "$\beta_1$"**:
   A bare "$\beta_1$" without identifying whether it represents graph cycle rank or simplicial nerve homology is strictly forbidden.
3. **No Substitution on Failure**:
   If simplicial nerve computation encounters a resource ceiling, record `nerve_status = "resource_failure"`, set $\beta_0^{\rm nerve} = \text{NA}$ and $\beta_1^{\rm nerve} = \text{NA}$. Under no circumstances may $\beta_1^{\rm graph}$ be substituted for $\beta_1^{\rm nerve}$.

## 6. Failure Semantics & Return Status Contract

| Case | `nerve_status` | $\beta_0^{\rm nerve}$ | $\beta_1^{\rm nerve}$ | $\beta_1^{\rm graph}$ | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Empty complex ($n_0 = 0$) | `empty_complex` | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | Return zeros |
| Node with empty support ($S(v) = \emptyset$) | `invalid_input` | NA | NA | NA | Raise `ValueError` |
| Non-integer external node ID | `invalid_input` | NA | NA | NA | Raise `ValueError` |
| Zero edges ($n_0 > 0, n_1 = 0$) | `success` | $n_0$ | $0$ | $0$ | Fast-path return |
| Chain condition $D_1 D_2 \neq 0 \pmod 2$ | `consistency_failure` | NA | NA | NA | Raise `HomologyConsistencyError` |
| Bound violation $\beta_1^{\rm nerve} > \beta_1^{\rm graph}$ | `consistency_failure` | NA | NA | NA | Raise `HomologyConsistencyError` |
| Simplex count exceeds ceiling | `resource_failure` | NA | NA | Valid | Log warning; preserve graph invariants |
| Normal valid complex | `success` | $n_0 - r_1$ | $n_1 - r_1 - r_2$ | $n_1 - r_1$ | Return exact Betti numbers |
