# Final endpoint and interpretation contract

## Axis I

The Axis-I endpoint is the exact joint observation-mismatch optimizer
corresponding to Belchi et al. Definition 9 when every point has an ordinary
cluster label. The release also applies an explicit noise-aware extension,
denoted `D_M^NA`, in which DBSCAN noise and outside-cover states are immutable
states rather than permutable ordinary labels. The historical ledger field
name `d_common_id` and machine tag `exact_joint_definition_9` are retained for
schema continuity; they do not remove this noise-policy qualification.

The optimizer minimizes one observation-level mismatch indicator jointly across
all cover elements; independently minimizing each bin is only an upper estimate
and is not released as the joint endpoint. When no special noise state occurs,
`D_M^NA` coincides with the ordinary Definition-9 objective.

`D_M^NA` is reportable only when the compared Mapper functions use the same
indexed realized cover of the compared domain and retain point identities.
Every successful evaluation must bind SHA-256 digests of both realized covers
and both filter definitions. Missing, malformed, or unequal digests fail
closed. A cover recomputed after subsampling or perturbation is not the same
cover. For Ball Mapper with frozen landmarks and no within-ball clustering,
label distance is algebraically zero; retained-edge and graph-change
diagnostics are used instead.

## Axis II

Construction status, coverage, non-convergence, and selection failure are
scientific outcomes. Undefined quality values remain null. Silhouette and
Davies–Bouldin values are aggregated only over eligible pullbacks.

## Axis III

Only same-domain, same-filter comparisons to an explicitly typed Reeb reference
are permitted. Conventional III-1 and III-2 support finite-sample recovery and
fidelity statements at their frozen designs. C5 F-Mapper and E2M III-3 support
only their stated finite cases. None is a convergence, isomorphism, or universal
recovery theorem.

## Cross-axis synthesis

Descriptive associations are not causal or inferential tests. Failures and null
estimands are not replaced by zeros. No universal winner or Pareto-optimal
configuration is claimed.
