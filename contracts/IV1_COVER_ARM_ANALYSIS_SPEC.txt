# IV-1 Cover-Arm Analysis Specification

**Status**: FINAL EXECUTED ANALYSIS
**Method**: Conventional Mapper only
**Design**: descriptive, R = 1; no cell-level inferential p-values

## Scope

IV-1 associates cover-induced Mapper-output distance with local clustering quality on three datasets, using the sealed I-B2/II-2 Conventional cover grid.

Datasets:

- `unit_circle_S1`
- `swiss_roll_with_hole`
- `digits_1797x64_scaled16`

Cover grid: 12 cells per dataset. Reference cell `L10_P030` (`n_intervals=10`, `overlap_frac=0.30`). The 11 non-reference cells in each dataset are the analysis spokes.

Planned evaluations: 11 spokes × 3 datasets = 33.

## Estimands

For spoke cover `C` and reference cover `C_ref = L10_P030` on the same dataset:

    D_C = D_COMMON(Mapper(C_ref), Mapper(C))

where `D_COMMON` is the type-restricted extended-persistence bottleneck distance on the Mapper 1-skeleton with node-mean lens filtration.

Primary association: Spearman `rho_s(D_C, Macro Silhouette)` on the complete-case mask of finite `D_C` and finite quality.

Secondary association: Spearman `rho_s(D_C, Macro Davies–Bouldin)` on the corresponding complete-case mask.

No p-values are reported. Ties and undefined quality remain explicit. Dual graph/nerve structural fields are stored for each spoke.
