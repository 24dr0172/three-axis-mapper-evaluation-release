Confirmatory reporting
    CLAIM_EVIDENCE_INDEX.csv retains the stable 35-claim architecture. Each
    confirmatory row has a nonblank estimand, controlling evidence,
    uncertainty statement, predeclared resolution rule, manuscript location,
    and terminal status. Resampling-based claims report the resampling unit,
    independent-cluster/stream count, interval method, and resolution rule in
    the manuscript. Exact finite-design and algebraic claims are labelled as
    such rather than assigned artificial resampling uncertainty.

C7 small-cluster disclosure
    The C7 block identifies beta_12, the stream as the resampling unit, 10
    independent stream clusters, the B=10000 nonparametric percentile
    bootstrap interval, the secondary CR1 t_9 interval, terminal state T2,
    and the limitation from only 10 stream clusters beside the intervals.

Tier and multiplicity policy
    Descriptive prose maps to the exploratory tier unless another tier is
    stated. Exploratory results are not described as hypothesis confirmation.
    The paper makes no familywise-significance claim across descriptive or
    exploratory analyses, and their intervals/diagnostic p-values are not an
    omnibus testing family. Confidence intervals, exact finite-design
    accounting, and predeclared decision rules are accepted without a blanket
    requirement to report p-values. IB4F diagnostic p-values do not adjudicate
    C3.
