Phase 0.3 — Manuscript inclusion rule
=====================================

Acceptance criterion
    No primary evidence can be omitted without an explicit scope reason.

Governing rule
    A retained claim is either (i) in the main text, (ii) in an appendix
    or the evidence map, or (iii) explicitly scoped out of adjudication.
    Silence is not a permitted status for any CLAIM_EVIDENCE_INDEX row.

Why a claim enters the main text
    It is needed to state a confirmatory terminal state, to exhibit a
    distinct axis-level failure mode, or to prevent a reader from inferring
    that a clean primary result was withheld.

Why a claim is appendix or evidence-map only
    It is supporting accounting, a finite-case diagnostic superseded by a
    larger replication, a descriptive sweep, or a calibrated analysis that
    the frozen contract already labelled exploratory.

Why a claim is scoped out of adjudication
    The endpoint was not replayed under the release convention, the rows
    are reconstructed rather than original persisted output, the numerical
    interval is vacuous, or the quantity is algebraic rather than empirical.

C1 special rule
    The original confirmatory C1 pattern is a failed confirmatory claim
    and must be stated as not observed. The Swiss alpha=0.05 pair may
    remain in the main text only as a descriptive example of axis
    disagreement, and only together with the full four-dose series.
    Language such as "most direct counterexample" is not permitted unless
    it is clearly labelled descriptive and supported by the complete dose
    series.
